# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good intent while addressing impact, and keep project
spaces focused on building a safe and useful developer tool. Harassment, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing another person’s private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for conduct
they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method shown on their
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow or
enforce this code fairly may be removed from the project’s moderation spaces.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
